Privacy Policy — Dvizh

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Maintainer: Sergey Shvetsov

Repository: https://github.com/Duckolly/dvizh

1. General principle

The app “Dvizh” is an offline tool for local physical activity tracking.

  • The app has no server.
  • The app does not require registration.
  • The app does not collect name, email, phone number, address, location, weight, or medical data.
  • The app does not use advertising.
  • The app does not use analytics.
  • The app does not use crash reporting.
  • The app does not send data to the developer.
  • The app does not automatically send data to third parties.

2. Locally processed data

The app may locally store on the user’s device:

  • activity type;
  • workout start date and time;
  • workout duration;
  • step count calculated from the device motion sensor.

These data do not leave the device.

In version v3.2.0, file export and sharing are disabled.

3. Permissions

Motion sensor access

The app may request access to motion sensors to count steps during an active workout started by the user. Data are processed locally and are not sent to a server.

Notifications

If the user enables notifications, the app may show a local ongoing notification indicating that recording is active. Notifications are not used for advertising and do not send data outward.

4. Data deletion

All data are stored only on the user’s device.

The user can:

  • clear workout history;
  • delete all app data inside the app;
  • uninstall the app, after which the operating system removes local app data according to its policy.

5. Children

The app is not intended for children under 14 without involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

6. Medical information

The app is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Step counting and activity tracking are approximate and depend on the device, sensors, and how the phone is carried.

7. Open source

The source code is publicly available so independent researchers can verify the absence of network requests, analytics, advertising, and hidden data transmission.

Water and home-screen widget / Вода и виджет (3.4.0)

Water is a manual daily log of 200 ml glasses, stored in a separate private SQLite database. The app stores workouts, daily steps, water and settings locally. Manual water logging works when activity recording is disabled. Water history can be cleared separately or with Delete all data.

Вода — ручной журнал стаканов по 200 мл с местной датой телефона. При смене суток открывается новый дневной итог без удаления истории. Все данные удаляются через настройки приложения.

A widget added by the user exposes local step and water totals to the system home-screen launcher. Anyone viewing the screen can see them. +200/−200 updates today's water without enabling the pedometer. The snapshot timestamp is shown; updates may be delayed by Android. No network, Health Connect or new permissions are used.

Добавленный пользователем виджет показывает локальные шаги и воду на рабочем столе. Данные видны наблюдателю экрана. Виджет не включает шагомер; для этого откройте приложение. Это описание исходников; публичная политика и данные владельца должны быть проверены перед публикацией.
